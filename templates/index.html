Abandoned but not Forgotten: NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon
Home

Over 180,000 to 225,000+ kilograms (roughly 400,000 to 500,000 pounds) of human-made equipment, spacecraft, and materials have been left on the Moon across various NASA, Soviet, and international missions. This includes: the bottom half of every Apollo Lunar Module, Saturn V S-IVB third stage rockets, Lunar Rovers (Electric Moon Buggies), U.S Surveyor Landers, Experimental (ALSEP) instruments, Mirror Arrays (that still reflect back to Earth), Hammers, Tongs, TV Cameras, Scoops, Sample Bags, a feather, Javelins, Flags, Plaques, Golf Balls, Family Photos, A Silicon Goodwill Disc, An Aluminum Sculpture, Ashes of Eugene Shoemaker, Food Packages, Wet Wipes, and roughly 96 bags of Human Waste. And much much more....
